A moment for yourself

October 19, 2026 · Listening to yourself · 3 min read

A little room to breathe

Have you ever noticed how quickly a quiet morning can fill up? One message arrives, then another. Someone needs a favor. There are plans to make, things to remember, and little responsibilities that seem to appear from nowhere. And before we know it, we've listened to everyone else without once asking ourselves how we're feeling. Funny how that happens, isn't it?

There's something beautiful about caring for others. But somewhere along the way, we can begin to believe that being available all the time is the same as being loving. We say yes when we need a pause. We smile when we're exhausted. We promise ourselves we'll slow down once everything is finished. But what if everything isn't meant to be finished before we allow ourselves to breathe?

Maybe you've felt that little tug inside. The one that says you need a quieter afternoon, a slower walk, or simply a few minutes without answering anybody. It's easy to ignore. We tell ourselves we're being dramatic, or selfish, or that we should be able to manage just one more thing. Yet our needs don't disappear because we've become good at overlooking them.

Look at the natural world for a moment. A garden doesn't bloom every day of the year. The ocean moves toward the shore and then pulls away. The sky can be bright one morning and full of clouds by afternoon. Nothing about those changes makes nature less beautiful. So why do we expect ourselves to be endlessly energetic, endlessly giving, endlessly ready?

What if today we gave ourselves permission to have a rhythm of our own? Not a perfect routine. Not another list of things to accomplish. Just a little honesty about what this moment is asking of us. Sometimes that answer will be movement and laughter and connection. Other times, it might be silence, a cup of tea, or an evening without plans.

And perhaps we can remember that two feelings are allowed to exist together. You can be grateful for your life and still need rest. You can love someone deeply and still need time alone. You can feel excited about a new chapter and a little frightened of what comes next. Being human is rarely as simple as choosing one feeling and leaving the others behind.

So today, maybe start small. Before saying yes, give yourself a moment to listen. Before reaching for your phone, notice the light in the room. Before filling every quiet space, ask whether you might actually enjoy it. These aren't grand gestures. They're gentle ways of remembering that your own voice deserves a place in your day.

Of course, there will be moments when life gets noisy again. Days when we forget to pause and find ourselves rushing from one thing to another. That's okay. Coming back to yourself isn't a test you can fail. It's something you can practice, again and again, with patience instead of judgment.

Maybe the beautiful thing isn't learning to control every part of our lives. Maybe it's learning to notice when we need to move forward and when we need to soften. To trust that rest doesn't erase our progress. To remember that changing our pace doesn't change our worth.

And if all you do today is take one honest breath, honor one quiet need, or choose one moment that belongs to you, let that be enough. You don't have to become someone new to deserve tenderness. You can begin exactly where you are. And whenever the world pulls you away from yourself, you are always allowed to come back.

Carry this into today

What small moment could you make room for today?
