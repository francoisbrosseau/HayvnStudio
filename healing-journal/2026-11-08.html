A moment for yourself

November 8, 2026 · Creativity & unmeasured joy · 3 min read

Some things are allowed to be only for the joy of them

At some point, a pleasant little interest can find itself being interviewed for a job. You enjoy taking photographs, and suddenly someone asks whether you will sell prints. You bake something lovely, and there is talk of a business. Even the peaceful act of arranging flowers can apparently require a strategy. The flowers, meanwhile, were having a perfectly good afternoon. ✨

It is wonderful when something we love becomes part of our work. There can be pride and possibility in that. But enjoyment also deserves a place where it does not have to produce an income, a finished object, or evidence of improvement. A few things in your life can remain deliciously difficult to justify on a spreadsheet.

Perhaps there is an activity you approach with genuine curiosity until the measuring begins. How good am I? Am I getting anywhere? Would somebody else find this impressive? Those questions can make a small pleasure feel like a narrow doorway with an examiner standing in it. Before long, you are checking your performance instead of noticing whether you are enjoying yourself. 🌙

Consider the freedom of doing something without preparing to present it. Draw a crooked bird. Sing the part of the song you actually remember. Spend an hour following a recipe because the process appeals to you, even if dinner arrives looking rather different from the photograph. There is a particular warmth in making something that needs no audience.

You can be interested without becoming accomplished.

That may feel surprisingly unfamiliar. Many of us learned to explain our time through usefulness. We can describe what an activity teaches, strengthens, saves, or earns, then feel more comfortable allowing it. Yet pleasure can be a sufficient reason on its own. You liked the colors. The movement felt good. You wanted to see what happened when you tried. These are complete answers. ⭐

Of course, time, money, space, and energy have real limits. An invitation to enjoy yourself should fit inside the life you actually have. You may have ten minutes and a pencil rather than an entire room for creating. You may prefer an activity that makes no mess because cleaning up would spoil the pleasure. Choose a size that feels welcoming. There is no required equipment for being curious.

If ambition is part of your enjoyment, keep it. Learn the difficult piece, practice the technique, enter the competition if that excites you. Just notice whether the goal still serves the experience, or whether the experience has become a task you dread. You can change the pace. You can return to the easy version. The imaginary talent committee can survive a day without receiving an update. 🔮

Today, give one interest a little shelter from evaluation. Leave the photograph unposted. Let the unfinished sketch rest on the table. Take the walk without recording the distance. Notice what you reach for when nobody is waiting to judge the result. Your life can hold things that matter because they delight, absorb, or quietly accompany you. Their value can remain close and personal, like a small light kept in a window simply because you love its glow. 🌟

Carry this into today

What would you enjoy doing today if there were nothing to prove afterward?
