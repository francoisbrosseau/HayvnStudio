A moment for yourself

November 16, 2026 · Friendship & gentle humor · 3 min read

Make a little room for shared laughter

A serious day can occasionally be interrupted by something wonderfully undignified. A dog carries a stick three times wider than the gate. Someone confidently sings the wrong words. A conversation takes a ridiculous turn, and suddenly two people are laughing harder than the situation seems to justify. For a moment, being together feels uncomplicated. Nobody needed to arrange a meaningful experience. 🌿

There is a particular kind of closeness in sharing what amuses you. You discover the tiny details another person notices, their unexpected comparisons, the expression that appears before they begin laughing. A private joke can become a small meeting place you return to across years. The reference may be completely incomprehensible to everybody else. This is part of its rather questionable charm.

Playfulness sometimes becomes harder to reach when life asks a great deal of us. Conversations fill with appointments, practical questions and the latest difficulty. Those subjects deserve attention. Still, you might notice that you miss another part of a friendship, the part where you are allowed to be silly without explaining what useful purpose it serves. 🌙

You could make a small opening for that part today. Share the photograph that made you smile. Mention the absurd detail from an otherwise ordinary errand. Ask a friend which completely unnecessary invention they would create if someone gave them a workshop and very little supervision. Let the question be light enough to answer badly. Sometimes the terrible answer is the beginning of the fun.

Humor needs care, especially when people are tired or carrying something tender. Notice who gets to enjoy the joke and who becomes its target. Shared laughter feels different when everyone can join freely. If someone grows quiet, you can slow down, listen or change direction. Affection becomes more visible when you respect what another person finds uncomfortable. ✨

And you never have to become cheerful on command. A friend may be grieving, worried or simply in a quieter mood. You might be the one who cannot find anything funny today. There can still be companionship in sitting together, talking plainly or letting the silence remain. An invitation to play should leave room for someone to keep their coat on and watch for a while.

Perhaps warmth begins with a little less self-consciousness. Allow an imperfect dance in the kitchen, a terrible drawing during a shared game, a dramatic reading of the instructions nobody understands. Choose company with whom these small experiments feel safe. You can enjoy being seen in an unpolished moment. The stars have witnessed plenty of awkward dancing and appear to be keeping the matter confidential. ⭐

Today, consider whether one connection in your life could use a little more play. Offer something small and see how it is received. You may get a smile, an exchange of increasingly ridiculous ideas or a gentle suggestion to try again after coffee. Let the response guide you. Serious things can receive their attention, while ordinary companionship keeps a little room for surprise. Sometimes the moment you remember fondly will be the one when nobody managed to finish the sentence because you were both laughing. 🌟

Carry this into today

Who might enjoy sharing one small, ridiculous moment with you today?
